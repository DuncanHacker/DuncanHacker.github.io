Privacy Policy — Buxly Last updated: October 28, 2026 1. About this policy This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Buxly, including its optional Buxly Pro subscription and Home Screen widgets. Developer and data controller: Bence Horvath Contact email: bence.horvath8@gmail.com This policy applies specifically to Buxly. Other applications from the same developer may use different services and have separate privacy policies. 2. Financial information stored on your device Buxly stores the information you enter, including transaction titles, amounts, categories, dates, notes, budgets, and recurring transaction settings, locally on your device. Your appearance preferences and personalized Home screen title are also stored locally. Buxly does not upload your financial records to a developer-operated server and does not require you to create an account. Device backups may contain app data depending on your Apple device and backup settings. Those backups are managed through Apple's services and your settings. 3. Home Screen widgets If you use Buxly widgets, the app makes monthly financial totals, your Home screen title, and subscription access information available to its widget extension through shared storage on your device. This allows the widget to display your financial summary. This sharing does not send your financial records to the developer or to an advertising service. 4. Advertising and tracking Buxly does not display advertisements and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Your financial records are not used for advertising or sold to third parties. 5. Purchases and subscriptions Optional Buxly Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple. Buxly uses Apple's StoreKit framework to check purchase and subscription information, such as the purchased product, subscription expiration, and revocation status, so it can provide access to paid features and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your full payment card details or Apple Account password. Apple may provide the developer with sales, transaction, and subscription reports. These are used to operate the subscription service and meet applicable accounting and legal obligations. For information about Apple's handling of purchase data, see: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/ Deleting Buxly does not cancel an active subscription. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. 6. Contacting support If you contact the developer by email, the information you voluntarily provide may include your email address, name, message, screenshots, and device or app details. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Please avoid including financial records or other sensitive information unless necessary. The legal basis for handling ordinary support requests is the developer's legitimate interest in providing support and resolving technical issues. 7. Retention and deletion Local app data remains on your device until you delete the relevant information or remove the app. Offloading the app may preserve its data, and copies in device backups may remain until you manage or delete those backups. You can delete individual transactions within Buxly. The developer cannot remotely access or delete financial records stored only on your device. Support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to resolve the request and related follow-up, then deleted when no longer needed, unless a legal obligation requires further retention. Financial records that must be retained for accounting or tax purposes are kept for the applicable statutory retention periods. Apple manages its own purchase records under its privacy policies. 8. Your privacy rights Your financial records are stored on your device and are not accessible to the developer. You can manage and delete individual transactions within Buxly. If you contact the developer for support, the developer receives the information included in your email. You may contact bence.horvath8@gmail.com to exercise your applicable data protection rights concerning that correspondence, including requesting access, correction, or deletion. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law. 9. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated when Buxly's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
